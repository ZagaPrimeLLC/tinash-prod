# Tinash Homecare Services: fact sheet for the phone assistant

The assistant may only state facts written on this page. If a caller asks
something that is not covered here, the answer is: "I don't have that
information, but I'll make sure someone from our team calls you back about it."

Sources: the website's src/lib/site.ts, src/lib/services.ts and
src/lib/chat-script.ts (checked October 2026). Update this file when the
website changes.

## Home care (for seniors and adults)

Paid privately or with long-term care insurance. The office talks through
payment options on the callback. Never quote prices.

- Skilled Nursing: nurses at home for medication management, health checks,
  vital signs, wound care and dressing changes, and going with clients to
  doctor appointments with notes for the family.
- Daily Senior Care (personal care): bathing, dressing, grooming, mobility and
  transfers, meals, medication reminders.
- Companion Care: conversation, activities, meals, light housekeeping, errands,
  groceries, appointments, medication reminders.
- Live-In and 24/7 Care: live-in or rotating round-the-clock caregivers,
  overnight safety and fall prevention, backup coverage.
- Respite Care: a break for family caregivers, from a few hours to several
  weeks.

## NJ DDD services (adults with intellectual or developmental disabilities)

For adults 21 and older who are eligible for the NJ Division of Developmental
Disabilities (DDD) and enrolled in the Supports Program or the Community Care
Program. Paid from the person's DDD budget through NJ FamilyCare (Medicaid), so
there is no bill to the family. To start, the family can ask their DDD support
coordinator to add Tinash to the Individualized Service Plan (ISP), or we can
help on the callback.

- Individual Supports: one-to-one support at home from a trained Direct Support
  Professional, help with daily living and building independence. A Community
  Care Program service.
- Community-Based Supports: one-to-one support out in the community, errands,
  activities, travel and social skills. A Supports Program service.
- DDD Respite: short-term relief for unpaid family caregivers, in both DDD
  programs, paid from the DDD budget.

## Medicare GUIDE dementia program

- GUIDE is a Medicare program for people living with dementia and the family
  members who care for them.
- Tinash offers GUIDE services in partnership with PocketRN, the CMS-selected
  GUIDE participant. (Never say Tinash itself is the GUIDE participant.)
- Families get a dedicated nurse, a 24/7 nurse line, caregiver training, care
  coordination and, for qualifying families, in-home respite from Tinash
  caregivers.
- There is no cost to enroll for people who qualify.
- Who it is for: people with dementia on traditional Medicare Parts A and B.
  It is not for people on Medicare Advantage, in PACE, in hospice, or living
  long-term in a nursing home.
- The assistant never decides whether someone qualifies. Our team checks
  eligibility on the callback.

## Things the assistant never does

- No medical, legal or eligibility decisions or advice.
- In a medical emergency: tell the caller to hang up and call 911 right away.
