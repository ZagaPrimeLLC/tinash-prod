Who qualifies?

  1. 1They have traditional Medicare (Original Medicare, not a Medicare Advantage plan)
  2. 2They have a dementia diagnosis (including Alzheimer's disease and related dementias)
  3. 3They are not currently enrolled in hospice

Not sure? Call us. We'll help you check eligibility in minutes, and a dementia evaluation can be arranged if your loved one hasn't been formally diagnosed.
